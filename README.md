## About Me

I'm a **Website Designer & Developer** specializing in business websites, corporate websites, landing pages, marketing websites, and personal websites.

My approach combines business research, strategic planning, thoughtful design, and modern web development to create digital experiences that serve real business objectives.

With **2+ years of practical experience** and **26+ websites built** across different commercial sectors, I've worked on Arabic and English projects and completed a four-month white-label collaboration with **Go Online**, a Gulf-based digital agency.

I work with both custom front-end development and visual website-building platforms, choosing the right approach according to each project's requirements.

## What I Build

- **Business & Corporate Websites** — Professional websites for companies and commercial activities.

## How I Work

I follow a structured, business-oriented approach to website development:

1. **Research & Discovery** — Understanding the business, audience, market, and competitors.
2. **Strategy & Planning** — Defining website structure, content hierarchy, and user journeys.
3. **Design & User Experience** — Creating clear, purposeful, and visually consistent interfaces.
4. **Development & Implementation** — Building the website using the most suitable development approach.
5. **Testing & Optimization** — Reviewing responsiveness, usability, performance, and technical quality.
